Questions

Before you ask.

How do I get a price?

Send a photo of the surface, its approximate size and what you want on it. For glass and projects we measure on site before fixing the price.

Does sun control film reduce heat?

Yes, it cuts solar heat and glare. The amount depends on the film and the glass. We will suggest a shade for your windows.

Can I give my own design?

Yes. Send a vector file (AI, CDR, PDF) or a clear image. We can also create or clean up the artwork.

Will a wrap damage my vehicle paint?

Quality vinyl applied on healthy factory paint can be removed later. Repainted or damaged paint is checked before we start.

Do you work on large projects for builders?

Yes. Send drawings and a quantity list. We quote boards, wayfinding, hoardings and glass film together and plan installation around your site schedule.
